## ✨ Features

- 🎁 **Smart Gift Matching** — Enter the person's name, age, and your relationship to get tailored gift ideas
- 👶🧑🧓 **Age-Aware Suggestions** — Curated categories for Children, Teens, Adults & Seniors
- 💑 **Relationship-Based Curation** — Different suggestions for Best Friends, Partners, Parents, Siblings, Colleagues & more
- 💰 **Budget Filters** — From budget-friendly (₹200+) to luxury (₹10,000+) options
- 🏷️ **Gift Badges** — Tags like Tech, Creative, Experience, Personal & Physical to quickly scan options
- 🎊 **Beautiful Animations** — Confetti bursts, floating particles, and smooth micro-interactions
- 📱 **Fully Responsive** — Works flawlessly on mobile, tablet, and desktop
- ⚡ **No Sign-Up Required** — Open and use instantly, no account needed

## 🎯 How It Works

1. **Enter Details** — Fill in the birthday person's name, age, and your relationship with them
2. **Pick a Budget** — Choose from Budget, Mid-Range, Premium, or Luxury tiers
3. **Get Suggestions** — Instantly receive 3–6 curated, relevant gift ideas
4. **Explore & Share** — Browse gift descriptions, tags, and budget ranges; share favourites

---

## 🧠 Gift Intelligence

The suggestion engine uses a structured **Gift Database** (`app.js`) that maps:

| Age Group | Relationships Covered |
|-----------|----------------------|
| Child (1–12) | Best Friend, Friend, Default |
| Teen (13–19) | Best Friend, Partner, Default |
| Adult (20–59) | Partner, Best Friend, Parent, Sibling, Boss/Colleague, Default |
| Senior (60+) | Spouse/Partner, Child, Default |

Each gift entry includes:
- **Emoji & Name** — At-a-glance identification
- **Description** — Why it's a great pick
- **Badge** — Category tag (tech / creative / personal / physical / experience)
- **Budget Tiers** — Price ranges across 4 budget levels
